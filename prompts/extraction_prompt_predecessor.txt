The objective is measurement, not summarization.

2. `inflation_outlook`
   * `-1`: the dominant forward-looking emphasis is disinflationary or points to downside inflation developments or risks.
   * `0`: the discussion is balanced, neutral, mixed, ambiguous, purely descriptive, or inflation is not substantively discussed.
   * `1`: the dominant forward-looking emphasis is inflationary or points to upside inflation developments or risks.
   * Classify the direction of the forward-looking inflation outlook or dominant expected trajectory, not merely whether the current level of inflation is high or low.

   * `1`: real economic activity, including GDP, output, demand, consumption, investment, recession, recovery, or closely related real-activity developments, is substantively discussed.
   * `0`: real economic activity is not substantively discussed.
   * A passing reference is not sufficient.
4. `growth_outlook`
   * `-1`: the dominant forward-looking emphasis is weakening activity, deterioration, recessionary conditions, or downside growth risks.
   * `0`: the discussion is balanced, neutral, mixed, ambiguous, purely descriptive, or growth is not substantively discussed.
   * `1`: the dominant forward-looking emphasis is strengthening activity, recovery, improving conditions, or upside growth developments.
   * Classify the direction of the forward-looking growth outlook or dominant expected trajectory, not merely whether the current level of economic activity is strong or weak.

6. `uncertainty_attention`
   * `1`: uncertainty, unpredictability, unusually wide risks, alternative scenarios, or uncertainty surrounding the economic outlook are substantively emphasized.
   * `0`: they are not substantively emphasized.
   * The mere occurrence of words such as "risk" or routine statements such as "risks are broadly balanced" is not sufficient.
